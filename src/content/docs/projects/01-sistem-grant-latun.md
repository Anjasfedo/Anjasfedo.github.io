## Overview

A comprehensive grant management system built for **LATUN**, an organization focused on coastal and marine conservation in Indonesia. The platform handles the full lifecycle of grant programs — from publishing calls for proposals to disbursement, reporting, and compliance.

## Interfaces

Two distinct interfaces serve different user roles:

**Admin Panel** — Full control over programs, proposals, partners, and legal documents. Includes dashboard analytics, proposal review workflows, document verification, and automated generation of legal documents (SK, SPK, RAB, Surat Pernyataan).

**Mitra (Partner) Portal** — Institutions register, complete their profile, upload verification documents, browse available grant programs, and submit proposals through a 6-step wizard. After approval, partners manage reports and self-assessments through the same portal.

## Key Features

- **Program Management** — Create and manage grant programs with configurable budgets, deadlines, KAK (TOR), and RAB templates
- **6-Step Proposal Wizard** — Guides partners through general info, beneficiaries, narrative, alignment, budget, and review before submission
- **Document Verification System** — 12 required document types per institution, with admin verification workflow before proposals can be submitted
- **Legal Document Generation** — Dynamic DOCX generation for SK Penetapan, SPK, RAB, and Surat Pernyataan
- **Reporting & Self-Assessment** — Partners submit periodic reports; self-assessment forms evaluate institutional capacity across leadership, HR, finance, and compliance
- **Interactive Tour System** — Context-aware guided tours that adapt to the user's stage in the grant lifecycle
- **Notification Engine** — Real-time notifications for proposal status changes, document verification, and deadline reminders
