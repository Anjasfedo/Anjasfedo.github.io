## Role

I mentored university students through a full-semester course on framework-based web development using **Laravel** (PHP).

## Course Content

The curriculum covered:

- **MVC architecture** and backend logic
- Database design with **MySQL**
- **Object-Relational Mapping (ORM)** — from basic CRUD operations to full-stack applications
- **RESTful API** design principles
- Server-side routing and Blade templating
- Clean separation of concerns and practical debugging strategies

## Teaching Approach

Beyond lectures, I facilitated hands-on lab sessions and code reviews, helping students diagnose complex issues and solidify their understanding of professional full-stack development workflows.
